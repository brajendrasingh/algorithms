Q11: What are primary shards and replica shards?
Ans: Shards are two types: primaries and replicas. Sharding is the process of dividing large dataset into smaller more manageable parts called shards.
No. of primary shards in an index is fixed at time of index creation , but No. of replica shards can be changed at any time.

When we index a document, Elasticsearch determines which shard to store it in based on a sharding algorithm which is typically using the document's ID or routing value
shard_num = hash(_routing/_id) % num_primary_shards
