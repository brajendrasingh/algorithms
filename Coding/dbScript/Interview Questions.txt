Q1: How do you find duplicate records like name, salaries, degrees?
Ans: SELECT degree, COUNT(*) AS count FROM employees GROUP BY degree HAVING COUNT(*) > 1;

Q2: How do you find the second highest salary?
Ans: SELECT DISTINCT salary AS SecondHighestSalary FROM employee ORDER BY salary DESC LIMIT 1 OFFSET 1;

Ans: SELECT e.*, d.* FROM departments d INNER JOIN employees e ON e.dept_id = d.dept_id;
